🔍 Translation & Localization Insights

Translating technical urban planning documentation requires localized institutional vocabulary, precision with spatial terminology, and readability for decision-makers:

Institutional & Administrative Terminology: Terms such as "Métropole du Grand Paris" were rendered as "Região Metropolitana de Paris" to match standard Brazilian geographical nomenclature. Expressions like "foncier public ou parapublic" were translated to "terrenos públicos ou parapúblicos", preserving the administrative nuances of French real estate governance.

Syntax & Readability: French analytical prose frequently employs passive voice and dense nominal constructions. The Brazilian Portuguese target text balances syntax for flow and clarity while maintaining strict accuracy regarding timelines, statistics, and project categorization (e.g., housing, artistic spaces, urban agriculture).
